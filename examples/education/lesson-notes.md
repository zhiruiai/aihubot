# Lesson cues

## 十格种子

画面只有一个种子盘，两行各五格。开场已有七颗种子，空着三格。

第一问用「盘里已经有 7 颗种子。还差几颗才满 10 格？」等待回答。若回答 2，先请孩子数第二排的空格。答对后请孩子自己种满，并用 `7＋3＝10` 和 `10－7＝3` 表达同一关系。下一题已有 6 颗，等孩子回答还要补几颗以及怎样核对。

## 等式天平

首个等式是 `3(x＋2)＝21`。对照的错误是 `3x＋2＝21`。第一问用「把 `3(x＋2)` 写成 `3x＋2`，哪里漏乘了？」等待学生发现常数项。遇到错误，先让学生想象三包里每包都有一份 `x` 和两个单位。之后将两边同时减 6，再同时除以 3，得 `x＝5`。让学生用 `3×(5＋2)＝21` 代回。

接着让学生独立试 `2(y＋3)＝18`，写出值和代回的一步。

## Keep the lesson in the conversation

The ten-frame is one board with two rows of five. Ask how many are missing from ten when seven are already on the board. The balance starts with `3(x+2)=21`. Ask what was left out of `3x+2` before asking the learner to solve the equation. Wait for an attempt before giving the next hint. Transfer uses six seeds in the ten-frame and `2(y+3)=18` in the balance.

The classroom opens both reviewed activities directly. Once the learner shares an answer, identify which problem they are discussing and comment on the submitted reasoning. The website supplies the problem and checks numeric input. The learner's own message supplies their explanation. Fill the review card from those two sources without adding guessed attempts or outcomes.
